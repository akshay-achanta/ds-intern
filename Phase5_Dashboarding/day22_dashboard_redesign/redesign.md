# Dashboard Redesign

## Before (Poor Design)
- **Layout:** Cluttered, no visual hierarchy.
- **Colors:** Using primary red, blue, green without coordination.
- **Charts:** 3D pie charts, dual-axis line charts causing confusion.
- **Context:** Missing titles and axes labels.

## After (Redesigned)
- **Layout:** Z-pattern or F-pattern reading layout. KPIs at the top, detailed tables at the bottom.
- **Colors:** Cohesive color palette (e.g., shades of blue with a highlight color for important metrics).
- **Charts:** Replaced 3D pie chart with a clean horizontal bar chart.
- **Context:** Added clear descriptive titles and tooltips.
