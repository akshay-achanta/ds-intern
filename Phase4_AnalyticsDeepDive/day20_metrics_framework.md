# Metrics Framework

## Business Goal
Increase overall company profitability and customer lifetime value (LTV).

## North Star Metric
**Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) / Total Monthly Revenue:** The absolute best indicator of our business growth and value delivered to customers.

## Supporting KPIs
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Total revenue divided by total orders.
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** Total marketing spend divided by new customers acquired.
- **Customer Retention Rate:** Percentage of customers who purchase again within 6 months.

## Leading vs Lagging Indicators
### Leading Indicators (Predict future outcomes)
- Daily Active Users on the platform
- Number of items added to cart
- Marketing email open rates

### Lagging Indicators (Reflect past performance)
- Total Revenue
- Churn Rate
- Return Rate
