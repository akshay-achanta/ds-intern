# Mini Project 1: Insight Deck

## Slide 1: The Problem
- The executive team lacks visibility into key sales drivers and underperforming segments, leading to inefficient marketing allocation.

## Slide 2: The Data
- We analyzed 5,000+ transactional records across multiple regions and categories, cleaning the data for accuracy.

## Slide 3: Key Findings
- **Finding 1:** 80% of revenue comes from just 2 regions.
- **Finding 2:** 'Electronics' has high revenue but also the highest return rate.
- **Finding 3:** Discounting beyond 15% does not significantly increase Average Order Value.

## Slide 4: Recommendations
- Target marketing spend on the top 2 regions.
- Investigate quality control for 'Electronics' to reduce return rates.
- Cap automatic discounts at 15%.

## Slide 5: Next Steps
- Implement the interactive dashboard for daily monitoring.
- Track return rates weekly to gauge quality improvements.
