# Mini Project 2: Operations & Logistics Analytics

## Domain
Logistics and Supply Chain Operations.

## Problem Statement
The operations team is experiencing high delivery delays and increasing shipping costs. We need to analyze delivery times, identify bottlenecks by warehouse and carrier, and optimize routing.

## KPIs
- **On-Time Delivery Rate:** Percentage of orders delivered on or before the estimated date.
- **Average Delivery Time:** Days from order to delivery.
- **Cost per Shipment:** Total shipping costs divided by number of shipments.

## Expected Outputs
- ETL Pipeline simulating data ingestion.
- Operations database.
- EDA and Analysis Notebook.
- Dashboard mockups.
- Final Presentation Deck.
